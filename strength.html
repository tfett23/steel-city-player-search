League Strength Tracker

Weekly roster-strength history based on season-to-date fantasy points per game.
Persistence: Lock Week updates this page's working copy and downloads a replacement strength-history.json. Replace the JSON file in the GitHub repository to make the lock permanent across devices. This page is intentionally unlinked from the public player-search page, but an unlinked GitHub Pages URL is not truly private.

Locked Weekly History

Rankings: Top 18 QB, Top 25 RB, Top 25 WR and Top 18 TE by season-to-date PPG through the selected week. Top 50 RB/WR is the sum of the Top 25 RB and Top 25 WR counts, matching the original spreadsheet logic. All 4 Positions is the sum of QB + RB + WR + TE counts (expected total 86) and does not double-count the RB/WR summary. A played 0.0 counts toward PPG; bye/DNP weeks do not. Players occupying a Sleeper reserve/IR slot at calculation time are excluded. Free agents are counted in the FA row.
